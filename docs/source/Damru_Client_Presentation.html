01Executive Summary

What Damru is, and the problem it solves for the business.

Damru is an integrated restaurant commerce and customer-engagement platform built for Damru By Namo. It brings together everyday restaurant commerce — browsing the menu, ordering, and checkout — with a full customer loyalty and rewards experience, all managed from one administration platform.

Customers can discover the menu and branches, place orders for delivery, pickup, or right at their table via QR code, manage their profile and addresses, and track their rewards — all from either the website or the dedicated mobile application. Every part of the experience is backed by the same platform, so a customer's Damru balance, order history, and rewards progress stay consistent no matter which device they use.

🌐

One Website

Full commerce & account experience, responsive across devices.

📱

One Mobile App

Native iOS/Android experience with matching functionality.

🛠️

One Admin Platform

Staff manage menu, orders, customers, and rewards from a single dashboard.

At the center of the customer experience is Damru — the platform's own reward currency. Customers earn Damru through everyday actions (placing orders, logging in daily, hitting milestones, referring friends) and redeem it directly against future orders. Every reward is issued and validated by the backend, so the balance a customer sees is always accurate and tamper-proof.

Why this matters for the business A rewards program built this way turns one-time diners into repeat customers — every reward mechanism (streaks, achievements, missions, referrals) is designed specifically to bring customers back and keep them engaged, without requiring a new app release every time a promotion changes.

The website and mobile application are two independent front doors into the same platform — they call the same backend, follow the same business rules, and show customers the same account, cart, and rewards state. Why a shared backend matters

Consistency

A customer's Damru balance, order history, and coupons look identical whether they check the website or the app.

One Source of Truth

Reward eligibility and order rules live in one place — not duplicated (and drifting apart) across two codebases.

Faster Change

A new promotion or reward rule configured once in admin applies everywhere immediately.

06Cart & Checkout

From selecting items to placing the order.

Cart
→
Address
→
Coupon / Damru
→
Payment Method
→
Order Confirmed

The cart lets a customer adjust quantities, apply a coupon code, and see a live price breakdown. Checkout then walks through delivery address selection (skipped entirely for table dine-in orders), an optional Damru redemption against the order total, and a final payment-method choice before the order is placed.

Every price shown to the customer — subtotal, discount, tax, delivery charge, and final total — is independently recalculated and verified on the backend when the order is actually created, so a customer can never be charged an amount the system didn't itself authorize.

Payment collection today Cash on Delivery is the fully working payment option today. UPI and Card are presented as selectable methods with their own screens, but neither is yet connected to a live payment processor — both currently show a "coming soon" message and direct the customer to choose Cash on Delivery.

An order can be Cancelled by staff at any point before delivery. Dine-in table orders use the same pipeline, with the final step relabeled "Served" and no delivery step.

Customers can follow their order's status from their profile at any time. Reaching the Delivered state is the single most important moment in the platform — it's the trusted signal that triggers Damru rewards, achievement progress, mission progress, and referral qualification, all at once.

08Damru Rewards System

The platform's own reward currency, and everything that earns it.

Damru is the platform's internal reward currency — never referred to as "coins" or points anywhere in the product. Customers earn Damru through a wide range of activities, see it building up in their wallet, and redeem it for real discounts at checkout.

Implemented reward sources

RewardWhen it's earned
Welcome RewardOnce, on successful registration
First Order RewardWhen a customer's first order is delivered
Birthday RewardEvery year, on the customer's date of birth
Marriage Anniversary RewardEvery year, on the customer's anniversary
Account Anniversary RewardEvery year, on the anniversary of joining
Daily Login RewardOnce per eligible day, amount increases with streak
Achievement RewardsOn unlocking a shopping, spending, engagement, or profile milestone
Mission RewardsOn completing a daily, weekly, monthly, or campaign challenge
Referral RewardsWhen a referred friend completes their first qualifying order
Loyalty Tier BonusOptionally, on upgrading to a higher loyalty tier

Every reward passes through one controlled engine Regardless of the source, every single Damru credit or debit goes through the same backend reward engine, is recorded permanently, and can never be issued twice for the same event — even if a request is accidentally repeated.

10Daily Streak

Rewarding customers simply for coming back.

Daily Visit / Login
↓
Backend Eligibility Check
↓
Streak Count Updated
↓
Damru Reward Credited

Customers see their current streak, their longest-ever streak, and exactly what tomorrow's reward will be — encouraging them to keep the chain going. The reward amount for each day in the cycle, what happens after the final configured day (restart, continue, or repeat weekly), and whether a missed day gets a short grace period are all controlled entirely by admin configuration — never hardcoded.

The backend determines eligibility from its own server clock — a customer cannot earn the reward twice by refreshing the page or calling the app repeatedly in one day.

Missions support daily, weekly, monthly, one-time, and limited-time campaign periods — each tracked toward order count, spending amount, login streak, or profile completion, depending on how admin configures it. Progress always comes from the backend; the app never decides on its own that a mission is complete.

13Referral Program

Turning happy customers into a growth channel.

Existing Customer Shares Their Code
↓
Friend Registers Using the Code
↓
Friend Completes a Qualifying First Order
↓
Referral Qualifies
↓
Both Customers Rewarded

Every customer gets their own short, shareable referral code and a ready-to-send invite link, with one-tap copy and native sharing on both website and mobile. The reward is not issued the moment a friend signs up — only after that friend completes a real, qualifying order, which admin defines by a minimum order value and (optionally) verification requirements.

Built-in abuse protection

  • A customer cannot refer themselves.
  • Each new customer can only ever be linked to one referrer.
  • Rewards for a single referral can only ever be issued once, even if the same event is somehow processed twice.
  • Admin can cap the number of successful referrals that earn a reward per customer.

14Loyalty Tiers

Recognizing a customer's long-term value.

Loyalty tiers give the platform's most valuable customers a clear status and extra benefits — entirely configured by admin rather than fixed in the app. A tier is defined by a name, a qualifying range (based on lifetime spend, completed orders, or lifetime Damru earned — admin picks one method for the whole ladder), a badge, and its benefits.

Bonus Damru Multiplier

Higher tiers can earn Damru faster on qualifying orders.

Exclusive Coupons & Early Access

Tier-specific offers where configured.

Optional Upgrade Bonus

A one-time Damru reward the moment a customer reaches a new tier.

Customers see their current tier, exactly how far they are from the next one, and the full tier ladder — all computed fresh from trusted backend data every time, never estimated on the device.

19Security

How customer trust is protected, in plain terms.

Protected Accounts

Passwords are never stored in readable form; sessions expire automatically.

Server-Verified Rewards

Every reward amount and eligibility check happens on the backend — never trusted from the customer's device.

Duplicate Protection

The same reward can never be issued twice for the same event, even under a repeated or retried request.

Secure Redemption

Damru redemption is tied to a specific, owned order and validated on the backend before any discount is applied.

Private Data Isolation

A customer's private coupons and order data are never visible to another customer.

Permission-Controlled Admin

Every staff action is checked against that staff member's assigned permissions before it's allowed.
